Please Choose a CV Type
Chronological:

The emphasis of this style is on a chronological listing of employment and employment-related experiences. The chronological CV is a good format for those with a consistent employment history.

Functional CV:

The functional CV highlights skills, experiences and accomplishments without identifying specific dates, names and places. This format is organized by functions or skills, which advertise the specific qualifications needed for an occupation. This CV works well for people changing careers.

Mixed CV:

The mixed CV brings together the best of both the chronological and functional CVs. It features a functional section that highlights skills, accomplishments and experiences. It also includes a chronological listing of employment, education and employment-related experiences. In a mixed CV, the chronological CV is enhanced with a section highlighting skills, accomplishments and experiences.
