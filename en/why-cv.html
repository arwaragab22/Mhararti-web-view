What is a CV?

and why is it important?

next

Tip

A curriculum vitae (CV) is a sort of visiting card that conveys personal and professional details to the prospective employer.

Its purpose is to attract the interest of the employer to the application and thus get invited to a job interview. Employers look at multiple CVs so it is important to take care to draft a good CV to highlight the skills and experience that are more relevant to the job you are applying for.
